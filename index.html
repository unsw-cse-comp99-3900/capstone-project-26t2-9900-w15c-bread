Dynamic History

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 August 2026

This policy applies to Dynamic History and Dynamic History (Client Trial), Confluence Forge apps developed as part of a UNSW COMP3900/COMP9900 capstone project.

1. Scope

Dynamic History helps authorised Confluence users view page-version history, compare versions, restore selected content, and add comments associated with page versions. This policy explains how the app accesses, processes, stores, and protects information when these features are used.

2. Information the app accesses

The app may access the following information from the installed Confluence site:

  • page identifiers, titles, current content, and historical page content;
  • page-version numbers, timestamps, and author information;
  • attachments referenced by page content;
  • Atlassian account identifiers and display names;
  • version comments entered by users and optional difference summaries; and
  • the permissions of the user invoking the app.

3. How information is used

The app uses this information only to:

  • load, render, and compare Confluence page versions;
  • identify version authors and render mentions;
  • preview and write a user-approved recovery as a new page version;
  • display attachments referenced by historical content; and
  • create, update, and display version comments.

4. Processing and storage

Dynamic History is hosted on Atlassian Forge. Page content, versions, attachments, and user information are retrieved through Atlassian APIs and processed within Atlassian Forge and the user's browser. The app does not use an external application database and does not send Confluence content to advertising or analytics services.

Version comments are stored as a Confluence page content property within the customer's Atlassian site. A stored comment may include the author's Atlassian account identifier, display name, creation time, comment text, and an optional difference summary.

5. Operational logs

Atlassian Forge may retain operational logs containing technical metadata, such as page identifiers, version numbers or counts, payload sizes, request status, and error messages. The app is not designed to write complete page content to its operational logs. Logs are used only for operation, troubleshooting, security, and support.

6. Disclosure and third parties

Dynamic History does not sell end-user data. The app relies on Atlassian Forge and Confluence to provide its functionality and does not disclose Confluence content to independent advertising, analytics, or data-broker services.

7. Retention and deletion

Confluence pages, versions, and attachments remain subject to the customer's Atlassian retention and deletion settings. Version-comment records remain in the applicable page content property until they are replaced or deleted. Uninstalling the app may not automatically delete these content properties.

Users seeking access, correction, or deletion should first contact their Confluence site administrator. They may also contact the Dynamic History team using the address below for assistance.

8. Security and permissions

Dynamic History requests only the Confluence permissions needed for its features. Requests to Confluence are made as the invoking user, so the user's existing Confluence permissions continue to apply. Site administrators control installation and removal of the app.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data-handling practices change. Material updates will be reflected by changing the effective date shown at the top of this page.
